Wei-Tao Lyu

Experimental astrophysicist working on superconducting detector technology for submillimetre astronomy. I design and characterise microwave kinetic inductance detector (MKID) arrays, integrate cold optics and cryogenic readout, and use polarimetric observations to study magnetic fields in molecular clouds.

Research Expertise

Detectors, optics, and polarimetry

01

MKID Design and Characterisation

Electromagnetic design and cryogenic characterisation of microwave kinetic inductance detector arrays, from resonator layout and notch-array readout to global fitting of resonator parameters.

02

Submillimetre Optics and Cryogenics

Optical design, alignment, and integration of room-temperature and cold optics, together with cryostat assembly and detector–optics co-alignment.

03

Submillimetre Polarimetry

Instrument design and calibration for polarimetric imaging, and interpretation of the data through Davis–Chandrasekhar–Fermi analysis of magnetised molecular clouds.
